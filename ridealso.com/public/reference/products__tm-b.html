DreamRide is a seamless integration of a series hybrid drive unit and software. Unlike other bikes, there’s no direct, mechanical connection between your pedaling and the movement of the bike. Instead, pedaling is converted into electricity by a generator and stored in the bike’s battery. Based on how you want to ride (e.g. smooth and easy versus hard and fast), ALSO software then tells the bike to go. DreamRide offers a first-of-its-kind experience, one determined entirely by you, not the road.

Propulsion Features

Dynamic Riding

There are two main ways to ride your TM-B—auto and manual. In auto, simply choose your preferred pedaling speed, or cadence (see below), and DreamRide will adapt to the road to maintain it. In manual, choose your gear like you would on a traditional bike, though with none of the sticking, slipping, or lag time. Because the TM-B’s gears are software-defined, the shifting experience is smooth, responsive, and immediate—like the perfect bike.

Assist

Assist is the extra juice the TM-B doles out to your pedal stroke, and it varies based on your chosen trim level. TM-B packs 5x assist while TM-B Performance cranks it up to 10x.

Throttle/Assist Boost

Sometimes it’s nice just to cruise, and, on the TM-B, cruise you shall. TM-B is equipped with a throttle that enables you to coast along, pedal-free, at up to 20mph. Try it on the scooter-inspired Bench top frame for a ride that’s optimally free and easy.

Due to regulations in some states and jurisdictions, throttle will act as an assist boost in those areas, temporarily increasing your assist multiplier while you hold it down.

Hill Flattening

Hill flattening crushes climbs for you. With this feature activated, DreamRide constantly monitors the grade of your ride and delivers extra torque to the wheels on ascents. The result? Hills take no more effort to ride than flats.

Regen

When you brake your TM-B you’re not just slowing down, you’re recharging your battery. Regen works to capture the kinetic energy normally lost to heat as you brake to keep your battery topped up. The amount of regen your bike applies is adjustable through your TM-B’s settings.

The Battery

The TM-B battery is built to automotive standards, delivering best-in-class performance and energy density. It’s available in two configurations—Standard, which delivers up to 60 mi. of range, and Large, which delivers up to 100 mi. It’s portable, with a neat, fold-out handle, and charges via USB-C at up to an exceptionally quick 240 watts. More than a battery, it can also be used as a high-capacity power bank for your devices.
