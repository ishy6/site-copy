DreamRide is a seamless integration of a series hybrid drive unit and software. Unlike other bikes, there’s no direct, mechanical connection between your pedaling and the movement of the bike. Instead, pedaling is converted into electricity by a generator and stored in the bike’s battery. Based on how you want to ride (e.g. smooth and easy versus hard and fast), ALSO software then tells the bike to go. DreamRide offers a first-of-its-kind experience, one determined entirely by you, not the road.

Propulsion Features

Dynamic Riding

There are two main ways to ride your TM-B—auto and manual. In auto, simply choose your preferred pedaling speed, or cadence (see below), and DreamRide will adapt to the road to maintain it. In manual, choose your gear like you would on a traditional bike, though with none of the sticking, slipping, or lag time. Because the TM-B’s gears are software-defined, the shifting experience is smooth, responsive, and immediate—like the perfect bike.

Assist

Assist is the extra juice the TM-B doles out to your pedal stroke, and it varies based on your chosen trim level. TM-B packs 5x assist while TM-B Performance cranks it up to 10x.

Throttle/Assist Boost

Sometimes it’s nice just to cruise, and, on the TM-B, cruise you shall. TM-B is equipped with a throttle that enables you to coast along, pedal-free, at up to 20mph. Try it on the scooter-inspired Bench top frame for a ride that’s optimally free and easy.

Due to regulations in some states and jurisdictions, throttle will act as an assist boost in those areas, temporarily increasing your assist multiplier while you hold it down.

Hill Flattening

Hill flattening crushes climbs for you. With this feature activated, DreamRide constantly monitors the grade of your ride and delivers extra torque to the wheels on ascents. The result? Hills take no more effort to ride than flats.

Regen

When you brake your TM-B you’re not just slowing down, you’re recharging your battery. Regen works to capture the kinetic energy normally lost to heat as you brake to keep your battery topped up. The amount of regen your bike applies is adjustable through your TM-B’s settings.

The Battery

The TM-B battery is built to automotive standards, delivering best-in-class performance and energy density. It’s available in two configurations—Standard, which delivers up to 60 mi. of range, and Large, which delivers up to 100 mi. It’s portable, with a neat, fold-out handle, and charges via USB-C at up to an exceptionally quick 240 watts. More than a battery, it can also be used as a high-capacity power bank for your devices. Ride Package

Choose from two ride packages based on the terrain you most frequently intend to ride.

Road

The Road package is best suited to those who intend to ride primarily on paved surfaces.

This package includes the following features:

  • 24” x 2.6” ALSO On-Road tires
  • Standard flat pedals

All Terrain

The All Terrain package is for those who intend to ride their TM-B off-road. The highlight of the package is Trail Ride Mode, a custom blend of ride parameters optimized for off-road riding. Parameters include: torque, regen profiles, wheel slip, traction control, top speed, and acceleration.


This package includes the following features:

  • ALSO x Kenda off-road 24 x 2.6 tires
  • Anodized aluminum off-road flat pedals
  • Trail Ride Modes (Auto and Manual)

Trims

TM-B comes in two primary trim levels, as well as a rotating line-up of limited editions. Trim levels determine a variety of features from battery configuration to suspension, all of which impact riding performance. All trims feature distinct colorways.

TM-B

TM-B comes equipped with a Standard Battery capable of delivering up to 60 mi. of range. It’s currently being offered in Gray Matter with Gray accents.

TM-B Performance

TM-B Performance offers a suite of upgrades that deliver an even more dialed-in and leveled-up riding experience:

  • Large Battery with up to 100 mi. of range
  • 10x assist (versus 5x)
  • Sport Ride Modes (Auto and Manual)
  • Upgraded suspension

TM-B Performance is currently being offered in Gray Matter with Ube accents.

TM-B Launch Edition

TM-B Launch Edition comes equipped with all of the same upgrades as TM-B Performance, and is offered in a one-of-a-kind colorway: Gray Matter with Ube/Sonic/Tales accents. Launch Edition bikes are also the first slated to begin shipping in spring 2026.

Cockpit

Choose from two cockpit configurations based on your preferred riding posture.

Sport

The Sport configuration is angled forward for a more active and aggressive riding posture. This configuration is best suited to exercise and active recreational riding.


Standard

The Standard cockpit configuration makes for a more upright, casual riding posture. This configuration is best suited to everyday rides, like commutes, trips to the store, etc.
